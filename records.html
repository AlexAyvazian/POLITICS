Data and methodology

Voting positions: Voteview congressional roll calls. Campaign finances: FEC candidate totals and limited itemized receipt/expenditure aggregates. Bill sponsorships: Congress.gov. Lobbying overlap: LobbyView research-coded records. Travel: official congressional disclosures. The page never assigns a lawmaker a financial total or a trip without an identified record.
