How to read this page

Final ruleAn agency has issued a final regulatory action. The official record controls the effective date and legal details.

Proposed ruleAn agency is proposing a change. It is not final policy yet.

Presidential actionA presidential document published through the Federal Register.

Legislative actionThe latest recorded action on a current-Congress bill or resolution. It is not necessarily enacted law.
