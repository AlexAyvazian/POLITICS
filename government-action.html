What these records do—and do not mean

A proposed rule is not the same thing as a final rule. A document posted to Regulations.gov may be a proposed rule, final rule, supporting material, or another docket document. GovInfo is an official publication repository, not an interpretation of policy. A bill listed by Congress.gov is legislation introduced in Congress; appearing here does not mean it passed or became law.

POLITICS labels the source on every record and links back to the official government record whenever a stable link is available.
